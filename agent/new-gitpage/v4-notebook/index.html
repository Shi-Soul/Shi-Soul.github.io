open questions一直在想p. 04

04Questions I keep coming back to反复在想的问题

  1. Is “general-purpose” a data problem, a prior problem, or a body problem?“通用”到底是数据问题、先验问题，还是身体问题？Humans learn with a body first and language second. Robots are being taught the other way round.人先有身体，后有语言；我们教机器人的顺序却反过来。
  2. What should the interface between a language model and a whole-body controller look like?大模型和全身控制器之间，接口应该长什么样？Text → motion is one answer (TextOp). I doubt it is the last one.文本到动作是一种答案（TextOp），但我不觉得是最终答案。
  3. Can a robot keep learning for its whole life without forgetting how to stand?机器人能不能终身学习，同时不忘记怎么站稳？LoopSR was my first attempt at this.LoopSR 是我第一次尝试回答。
  4. What can phenomenology teach roboticists about perception and skill?现象学能教给机器人研究者什么？Merleau-Ponty thought skill lives in the body, not in rules. That is, oddly, an RL claim.梅洛-庞蒂认为技能在身体里而不在规则里，这听起来很像强化学习。
